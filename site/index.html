THE FAILURE MODE

A model can be right
and still be unaccountable.

In research review, the cost is not only a bad answer. It is an answer nobody can reconstruct, challenge, or safely revise after the evidence changes.

01

Unsupported certainty

An excerpt cannot establish a claim, but the model fills the gap anyway.

02

Instructions in the source

“Ignore previous instructions” is data to inspect—not authority to obey.

03

History disappears

A changed finding silently overwrites the original decision and rationale.
